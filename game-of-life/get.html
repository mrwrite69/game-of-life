Install it properly

📱

iPhone & iPad

YOU'RE HERE

Add it to your Home Screen and it runs full-screen with its own icon — the same as an App Store app, without the App Store.

  1. Open this page in Safari (it has to be Safari, not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The beagle shows up on your Home Screen.
One difference to know about: iPhone doesn't let web apps send scheduled notifications, so the 11:11 daily spark and the night-owl check-in won't buzz your phone. Everything else — points, streaks, missions, quests, boss battles, cloud sync — works exactly the same.
🤖

Android

YOU'RE HERE

Install it straight from Chrome — same full-screen app, own icon, nothing to sideload.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (sometimes Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm. The beagle shows up in your app drawer.
Downloadable APK coming soon. There's a native Android build with working notifications — it just isn't packaged for release yet.
💻

Mac

YOU'RE HERE

Works in any browser right now. In Chrome or Edge you can also install it properly — look for the ⊕ icon in the address bar, or the menu ⋮ → Install Game of Life. It then opens in its own window with its own Dock icon.

Native Mac app coming soon. There's a desktop build in the works — it needs signing before it's worth handing out.
